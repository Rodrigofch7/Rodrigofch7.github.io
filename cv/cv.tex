\section{Projects}

\cvproject{NYC Property Tax Assessment}
          {Python · LightGBM · scikit-learn}
          {\href{https://github.com/Rodrigofch7/nyc_property_taxes_local}{[GitHub]} \enspace \href{https://nycpropertytaxes.streamlit.app/}{[Live Dashboard]}}
          {End-to-end ML pipeline classifying all 1.1M NYC properties (FY2015–2027) as over-, under-, or fairly valued, so a review team can target the properties most likely to be misassessed instead of auditing at random. Designed a novel 6-level peer-group labeling methodology and engineered 138 features (assessment trends, YoY acceleration, ZIP and building-class aggregates, OLS projections); LightGBM reached 87.6\% accuracy and 0.867 F1-macro against a 57\% majority-class baseline. Runs reproducibly at full scale via memory-safe chunked ingestion and checkpoint-resumable tuning.}
